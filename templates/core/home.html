How Digicow works

We train

Our coordinator sets the topics and our trainers travel to the county to teach farmers on the ground.

2

We follow up

Our Q&A team calls farmers to confirm they attended, hear their feedback and collect their questions.

3

We supply

Farmers can order quality farm products through the trainer, and every order is tracked digitally until delivery.

How we reach farmers

Advice that starts in the community

Our field trainers meet farmers where they live and work, bringing practical learning closer to local communities across more than eight counties.

Training topics focus on useful farm practices. Trainers explain and discuss them in person, then our Q&A team follows up by phone to hear what farmers learned, collect questions and pass feedback back to the team.

That follow-up helps us keep advice connected to farmers' real experiences—not just the training day.
