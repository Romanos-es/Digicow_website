Field training

Trainers are sent to counties to teach farmers on topics chosen by our training coordinator, so every session covers what farmers need most.

Q&A follow-up

After each training, our Q&A team phones farmers to confirm attendance, gather feedback and pass their questions back to the trainers.

Digital orders

Trainers record farm product orders on the Digicow system, where they are approved, tracked and delivered.
